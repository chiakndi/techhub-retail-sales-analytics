# TechHub Retail — Project Summary

**Goal:** Examine retail sales, profitability, customer behavior, supplier performance and acquisition channels in Tableau, and demonstrate a reproducible analytics workflow in Google Colab.

**Original internship report:** Tableau figures for category revenue/profit, monthly performance, product treemap and customer scatter plot; findings on category differences, Q4 seasonality, channel variation and product aging.

**Reproducible example:** Five linked synthetic CSV datasets, Python notebook and derived analysis tables. All numerical revenue, profit and value totals shown in this repository are demonstrations, not original company results.

**Skills:** Tableau, business intelligence, KPI design, data validation, Python, pandas, Matplotlib, profitability analysis, customer segmentation and analytical storytelling.

**Next steps:** Obtain original dataset and Tableau workbook (where sharing is permitted) to validate original KPIs and reproduce the full dashboard interactively.
